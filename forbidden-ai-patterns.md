# Forbidden AI Writing Patterns

Check every draft against this list before sending. Add a new entry when a new tell appears.

1. **False contrast.** “That’s not X, that’s Y.” Cut the setup. Keep the claim. Write “They’re stalling,” not “That’s not compliance, that’s stalling.”
2. **Fragment pairs.** Do not join two clipped phrases for rhythm, such as “Fast. Simple.” Write one normal sentence.
3. **Metaphor with no instruction.** Replace “Less a hammer, more a scalpel” with a clear action.
4. **Self-congratulation.** Delete lines such as “And that matters,” “That’s the part everyone misses,” and “Which is exactly the point.”
5. **Unexplained analogy.** Explain the idea in plain words. Do not write lines such as “It’s the Excel of AI agents.”
6. **Throat-clearing.** Delete lines such as “Here’s the thing,” “Let me be clear,” and “The truth is.”
7. **Reflexive triads.** Do not default to groups of three for cadence. Use the real number of reasons.
8. **Fake-precise ranges.** Do not invent a range. Give a measured value or label the range and its source.
9. **Recap endings.** Do not end with “In short” or “At the end of the day.” Stop when the point is complete.
